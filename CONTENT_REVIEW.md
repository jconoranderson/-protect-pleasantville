# Redesign content review — September 18, 2026

The supplied redesign brief sets the campaign direction. The implementation removes the former EIS/Positive Declaration demands and focuses on the zoning decision, protest eligibility, public comment, and independent analysis.

## Corrections applied

- **Protest threshold:** [NYS Village Law § 7-708](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VIL/7-708) describes owners representing at least 20% of land area within specified categories, not 20% of neighboring owners. Its three-fourths threshold means four affirmative votes on a five-member Board. Two NO votes would defeat that amendment if the threshold applies; they do not permanently prohibit future proposals. Local § 185-58H and parcel-specific application still need organizer/legal confirmation.
- **EAF is not the only record:** The [Village project repository](https://www.pleasantville-ny.gov/2028/Lennar-Zoning-Amendment-Townhouse-Propos) contains technical memoranda, plans, traffic studies, and other materials. The site does not claim the Board has only an EAF.
- **Office occupancy:** The [August 5 applicant memorandum](https://www.pleasantville-ny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1766/Campus-Drive-Technical-Memorandum), p. 2, describes the smaller office as occupied. The campaign’s entirely vacant/zero-baseline characterization conflicts with this filing and was not repeated as fact.
- **Traffic arithmetic:** 895 / 6,651 × 100 = 13.4566%, which rounds to 13.5%, not 13.4%. The delivery allowance, ITE rate/method, route assignment, baseline, and possible double counting need support. The site distinguishes this scenario from an observed or independently verified net increase.
- **Crash statistic:** 391 / 1,947 ≈ 20.1%. Dates, geographic scope, and deduplication of intersection records are needed. The dashboard link alone does not establish the brief’s totals.
- **Water:** Applicant Table 26 supplies the 33,660 GPD design estimate. Storage volume is not a stormwater discharge rate. No legal conclusion that a “TBD” entry invalidates the filing is asserted.
- **Construction:** The page asks for excavation, demolition/asbestos, dust, and vibration safeguards without claiming a single EAF answer automatically waives separate permit requirements. Site-specific soil evidence remains outstanding.
- **Regional comparison:** The linked HGAR account describes proposed Mount Pleasant changes, not adopted regulations.
